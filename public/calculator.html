Home & Auto Loan EMI Simulation

Floating-rate retail loans are mandated by the RBI to be linked to an External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR). When the MPC changes the Repo Rate, banks are required to pass through the change within 3 months.

  • -25 bps Rate Cut: Saves ~₹812/month on a ₹50 Lakh 20-year home loan.
  • -50 bps Rate Cut: Saves ~₹1,617/month or reduces tenure by ~17 months.
  • Tenure Shortening Option: Keep EMI constant to save over ₹1.95 Lakh in lifetime interest.

Bank Fixed Deposit (FD) Yields

Commercial bank deposit rates adjust in response to systemic banking liquidity and the policy repo rate.

  • Quarterly Compounding: Standard across SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank.
  • Senior Citizens: +0.50% additional yield across 1-5 year tenures.
  • Real Inflation-Adjusted Return: Assesses yields against MoSPI headline CPI inflation (~3.65%).

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon do banks reduce loan EMIs after a repo rate cut?

Under RBI rules, banks reset EBLR-pegged retail loans at least once every 3 months following a policy rate revision.

Why did my bank increase tenure instead of reducing EMI?

Banks typically default to tenure adjustments to maintain constant cash inflows, but borrowers can explicitly request an EMI reduction.
