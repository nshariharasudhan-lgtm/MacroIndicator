Data Methodology & Standards

MacroNest compiles data strictly from official releases of statutory agencies, central banks, and government ministries.

Compilation & Verification Protocols

  • Primary Ingestion: Statistics are transcribed from official gazettes and press bulletins.
  • Provisional vs Final: Early prints are noted as provisional and updated when revised.
  • EBLR Benchmark Rules: Floating loans issued since 1 Oct 2019 are linked to external benchmarks (RBI Repo Rate). Older loans sanctioned under MCLR or Base Rate reset on scheduled contractual dates.
