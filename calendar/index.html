Frequently Asked Questions on India Economic Releases

When does the RBI Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) announce repo rate decisions?

The RBI Monetary Policy Committee meets bimonthly (six times a year), typically in February, April, June, August, October, and December. The policy resolution, interest rate decisions, and growth/inflation projections are announced at 10:00 AM IST on the final day of the 3-day MPC meeting.

On what day is India's Retail CPI Inflation data released?

India's headline Consumer Price Index (CPI) retail inflation is published monthly by the National Statistical Office (NSO), Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), on the 12th of every month at 4:00 PM IST. If the 12th falls on a weekend or gazetted central government holiday, MoSPI publishes the release on the next working day.

When is India's quarterly GDP growth data published?

Quarterly Gross Domestic Product (GDP) and Gross Value Added (GVA) estimates are published by MoSPI on the last working day of May (Q4 Jan-Mar), August (Q1 Apr-Jun), November (Q2 Jul-Sep), and February (Q3 Oct-Dec).

When does the RBI publish India's Current Account Deficit (CAD) and Balance of Payments?

India's Balance of Payments (BoP) and Current Account Deficit data are compiled and published quarterly by the Reserve Bank of India with approximately a one-quarter lag. Q1 (Apr-Jun) data is published towards the end of September, Q2 (Jul-Sep) towards the end of December, Q3 (Oct-Dec) towards the end of March, and Q4 (Jan-Mar) towards the end of June.

How frequently are India's Foreign Exchange (Forex) Reserves announced?

The Reserve Bank of India releases official Foreign Exchange Reserves figures weekly in its Weekly Statistical Supplement (WSS) every Friday at 5:00 PM IST, covering total reserves ($bn), foreign currency assets (FCA), gold reserves, and Special Drawing Rights (SDRs).
