About MacroNest

MacroNest is an independent macroeconomic data monitoring platform designed to provide transparent, accessible, and structured tracking of Indian and global economic indicators.

Our Mission

Economic indicators shape borrowing costs, investment returns, inflation expectations, and business decisions across India. MacroNest compiles and structures this information from official statutory feeds into a unified dashboard, accompanied by practical simulation tools like the RBI Repo Rate Loan & Savings Calculator.

Official Sources

Data on MacroNest is compiled from official sources such as the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN), Office of the Economic Adviser, Ministry of Commerce & Industry, plus other public sources.
